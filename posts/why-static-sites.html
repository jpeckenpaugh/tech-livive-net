Why static sites still win

October 4, 2026

A static site is just files. GitHub Pages serves them straight from a repository, so there is nothing to keep running and nothing to patch at 2 a.m.

The best infrastructure is the infrastructure you do not have to operate.

What you give up

Dynamic features: comments, search, personalization. Most of those have static or third-party answers, and most blogs never needed them.

What you get back

Speed, security, and longevity. A folder of HTML from 2010 still opens today. That is not true of most content management systems.
